The quarterly BAS checklist for sole traders

For GST-registered sole traders lodging quarterly · Last updated October 2026

Work through this top to bottom every quarter and nothing gets missed. It takes about an hour the first time, less each quarter after that.

One-time setup, do this first

  • ☐ Confirm your reporting basis: cash or accruals. Cash is the default choice for most sole traders and matches your bank statement.
  • ☐ Keep business money in a separate account. Mixing personal and business transactions is the single biggest time sink in BAS prep.
  • ☐ Not registered for GST yet? You only lodge a BAS once your turnover passes $75,000. Until then, skip this page entirely.

Gather, before you touch any numbers

  • ☐ All sales invoices for the quarter, or your income records if you invoice as you go.
  • ☐ Expense receipts and invoices, especially anything paid by cash or personal card.
  • ☐ Business bank and credit card statements for the full quarter.
  • ☐ Your previous BAS, for comparison.
  • ☐ Your PAYG instalment notice, if the ATO has put you on instalments.

Work the numbers

  • ☐ G1: total sales including GST, and including GST-free income.
  • ☐ 1A: GST collected. Divide each GST-inclusive taxable sale by 11.
  • ☐ 1B: GST paid on business purchases. Divide GST-inclusive purchases by 11, but only the business-use share of mixed purchases.
  • ☐ W1 and W2: almost always nil for a sole trader with no employees. Leave blank, don't put zero transactions there.
  • ☐ T7: enter your PAYG instalment amount if the ATO has you on the instalments system.

Sanity checks before you lodge

  • ☐ Pick one invoice and redo its GST by hand. If the tool or spreadsheet disagrees with you, stop and find out why.
  • ☐ Compare 1A with last quarter. A similar figure is normal. A sudden jump or drop needs an explanation before you lodge, not after.
  • ☐ GST-free income appears in G1 but must not appear in 1A.
  • ☐ No GST claimed on bank fees, interest, wages, or super. None of them carry GST.
  • ☐ Check quarter boundaries. On the cash basis, a payment counts in the quarter the money moved, not the invoice date.

Lodge and pay

  • ☐ Check the deadline on our BAS due dates page.
  • ☐ Lodge through ATO online services via myGov, or through your registered agent.
  • ☐ Pay by the due date. If you can't pay in full, lodge anyway and contact the ATO about a payment plan. Lodging late costs more than owing.
  • ☐ Save the lodgement confirmation.

After lodging

  • ☐ File everything. The ATO requires records be kept for five years.
  • ☐ Note anything you'll do differently next quarter, like chasing receipts weekly instead of quarterly.
  • ☐ Set a phone reminder for the next due date.
