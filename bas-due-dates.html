Why Q2 says 1 March, not 28 February

28 February 2027 falls on a Sunday. When a due date lands on a weekend or public holiday, it moves to the next business day. That's the one quirk in the table worth knowing, because it catches people who set a recurring reminder for the 28th.

If you lodge monthly

Monthly lodgers report by the 21st of the following month, every month. Most sole traders under $20 million turnover lodge quarterly, so if you never chose monthly, the quarterly table is yours.

A note on agent extensions

If you lodge through a registered tax or BAS agent, their lodgement program usually gives several extra weeks beyond the dates above. If you lodge yourself through myGov, the table is your deadline, full stop.

What happens if you're late

  • A failure-to-lodge penalty can apply, charged per 28-day period late. For a small business it starts at $330 per period and rises the longer you leave it.
  • Interest accrues on any unpaid amount from the due date.
  • The ATO doesn't chase you before the deadline. No reminder arrives. The dates above are on you.

The rule that saves money: always lodge on time, even if you can't pay. The penalty is for late lodgement, not for owing. Lodge, then contact the ATO to arrange a payment plan.
